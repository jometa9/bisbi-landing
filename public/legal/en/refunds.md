# Refund Policy

This policy explains when Bisbi (operated by **IPTRADE COPIER LLC**, Delaware, USA) issues refunds. Refunds, when applicable, are processed by IPTRADE COPIER LLC back to the original payment method.

---

## General policy

**All sales are final. We do not issue refunds except in the specific cases detailed below.**

### Why are there no refunds by default?

Because we offer:

- A **free plan** so you can try Bisbi before paying.
- Detailed product information on the website.
- Cancellation at any time (cuts off future charges).
- Technical support to resolve issues.

---

## Cases where there is NO refund

- Change of mind.
- You forgot to cancel before renewal.
- You did not use the software during the paid period.
- Technical problems on your side (internet, microphone not working, outdated operating system, missing OS permissions).
- Configuration or usage errors.
- Cancellation mid-billing-cycle.
- Plan downgrades (account credit applies, not a cash refund).
- Dissatisfaction with transcription quality when used outside recommended conditions (low-quality microphone, heavy ambient noise, unsupported languages or accents).
- Violations of our Terms of Use.

---

## Exceptions (refund may be available)

We may issue a refund, at our discretion, in these cases:

### 1. Billing errors

**Our mistake** — We charged you incorrectly, twice, or after you cancelled.

### 2. Unauthorized charges

Your payment method was used without your authorization (proof required).

### 3. Legal requirements

Where required by law (for example, EU consumer rights — see below).

---

## EU consumer rights

**14-day right of withdrawal (EU / UK / Switzerland residents):**

You may withdraw from the purchase within 14 days without giving any reason.

**However:** if you activate and use the software during that 14-day period (transcribing words consuming the paid plan), you waive the right of withdrawal due to the digital nature of the service.

**Note:** the free plan lets you try Bisbi risk-free before purchasing the Pro plan.

## How to request a refund

**1. Contact support first** to try to resolve the issue:
Email: [hello@bisbi.io](mailto:hello@bisbi.io)

**2. If it is not resolved, send the refund request:**
Email: [hello@bisbi.io](mailto:hello@bisbi.io)
Subject: "Refund request"
Include:

- The email of your account.
- Date and amount of the transaction.
- Reason for the request.
- Supporting documentation, if applicable.

**3. Review process:**
We review the case within 10 business days and communicate the decision.

---

## Refund processing

**If approved:**

- Refunded to the original payment method.
- Processing takes between 5 and 10 business days.
- Your subscription is cancelled.
- You must uninstall the software.

---

## Chargebacks

**Contact us first** before initiating a chargeback.

**Initiating a chargeback without notice:**

- Immediately suspends your account.
- Revokes access to the software.
- May trigger an administrative fee of USD 25 if we win the dispute.

**Fraudulent chargebacks** may lead to permanent ban and legal action.

---

## Plan changes

**Plan downgrades** generate account credit (not a cash refund). The credit is applied automatically to your future invoices.

**We do not issue cash refunds for plan changes.** The proration system ensures you are not paying for unused time.
