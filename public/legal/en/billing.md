# Billing Policy

This policy explains how Bisbi (operated by **IPTRADE COPIER LLC**, Delaware, USA) bills and charges for the use of its software and services. Invoices and receipts are issued in the name of IPTRADE COPIER LLC.

---

## Subscription plans

### Free plan

- **Cost:** $0.
- **Usage:** dictation limited to 2,000 words per month (the current limit is also shown inside the app and on the dashboard).
- **Includes:** all the main app features (local transcription, configurable shortcut, local history).
- **Duration:** no expiration. You can use it for as long as you want within the monthly limit.

### Pro plan

- **Usage:** **unlimited** dictation.
- **Billing:** monthly or annual (with a discount over the monthly rate).
- **Support:** priority email support.

Current prices and billing options are published on bisbi.io and in the checkout flow inside the app.

---

## Billing cycles

- **Monthly:** charged on the same day of each month.
- **Annual:** charged once a year, with a discount over the monthly option.
- **Automatic renewal:** subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.

---

## Payment methods

We accept:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover).
- Digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay).
- Bank transfer (annual plans only).

**Payment processing:** all payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your card data.

---

## Automatic renewal

All subscriptions renew automatically at the end of the billed period.

**Reminders:** we send an email 7 days before each annual renewal.

**You can cancel at any time** from the billing section of your account or by writing to [hello@bisbi.io](mailto:hello@bisbi.io).

---

## Cancellation

**How to cancel:**

- From the billing section inside your account.
- Or write to [hello@bisbi.io](mailto:hello@bisbi.io).

**After cancelling:**

- Access to the paid plan continues until the billed period ends.
- We do not charge you for the next period.
- We do not issue a refund for the current period (see Refund Policy).
- At the end of the period, your account reverts to the free plan and the corresponding limits apply.

---

## Plan changes

### Upgrading

- Takes effect immediately.
- Charged on a prorated basis for the remaining time of the current period.
- New features are activated instantly.

### Downgrading

- Takes effect at the end of the current period (or earlier, if coordinated with support).
- No cash credit is issued when moving to the free plan.

---

## Billing disputes

**Contact us first** before initiating a chargeback: [hello@bisbi.io](mailto:hello@bisbi.io).

**Chargebacks:** opening a chargeback without contacting us first will result in immediate suspension of the account.

---

## Price changes

We may change prices with 30 days' prior notice. Changes apply at your next renewal. If you do not agree, you can cancel before the renewal.
