## System permissions

To work, Bisbi needs operating-system permissions:

- **Microphone:** to capture your voice (Mac and Windows).
- **Accessibility (macOS only):** to simulate Cmd+V and paste the text into the active app. On Windows, pasting is performed via the standard system APIs and does not require an additional permission.
- **Clipboard access:** to copy the text before pasting it.

**You decide whether to grant these permissions.** Bisbi only accesses the microphone during recordings that you initiate, either by holding the shortcut (push-to-talk) or by enabling hands-free mode (which records until you stop it with the same shortcut). The clipboard is only used to deliver the transcribed text to the active app.

If you revoke system permissions, some features (such as automatic pasting) will stop working.
