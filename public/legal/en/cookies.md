# Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Bisbi (operated by **IPTRADE COPIER LLC**) uses cookies and similar technologies on its website.

---

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files saved on your device when you visit our website. They are used to maintain your session and improve your browsing experience.

---

## Cookies we use

### Essential cookies (always active)

Required for the site to work correctly:

- Session and login management
- Security and authentication

These cookies are strictly necessary and cannot be disabled.

### Marketing and analytics cookies

Used for advertising and metrics:

- **Meta (Facebook) Pixel** — Conversion tracking and ad performance measurement.

---

## Third-party cookies

**Meta (Facebook) Pixel:**

- Measures conversions and ad performance.
- [Meta's privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/)
- To opt out: [Facebook ad preferences](https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences)

**Stripe:**

- Cookies used to process payments.
- Required to manage subscriptions.
- [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy)

---

## Browser settings

Most browsers let you:

- Block all cookies.
- Delete existing cookies.
- Allow cookies only from specific sites.

**Note:** if you block essential cookies, the site is likely to stop working correctly.

---

## Bisbi on your computer

Bisbi is a desktop application for Mac and Windows. The application installed on your device **does not use cookies**: cookies only apply to the bisbi.io website. Preferences and transcription history are stored locally on your computer, not in cookies.
