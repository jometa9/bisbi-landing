# Complaints Policy

We care about delivering an excellent service. If you have a complaint, we want to hear it and resolve it fairly.

---

## How to submit a complaint

### By email (recommended)

**Email:** [hello@bisbi.io](mailto:hello@bisbi.io)
**Subject:** "Complaint - [Brief description]"

### By postal mail

IPTRADE COPIER LLC (Bisbi)
131 Continental Dr, Suite 305
Newark, DE 19713, United States

---

## What to include

**Required information:**

- Name and email associated with your account.
- Product you are using (Bisbi for Mac or Windows) and installed version.
- Clear description of the issue.
- When it happened.
- How it affected you.
- Resolution you are seeking.

**Supporting evidence (if applicable):**

- Screenshots or error messages.
- Transaction IDs.
- Prior related correspondence.
- App logs (you can export them from Bisbi → Settings → Support).

---

## Our process

### Step 1: Acknowledgement (within 24 hours)

We confirm receipt of the complaint and assign it a case number.

### Step 2: Investigation (3 to 7 business days)

We review the situation and, if we need additional information, we contact you.

### Step 3: Resolution (within 10 business days)

We communicate the conclusions and the proposed solution.

**Complex complaints** may require more time. In that case, we keep you informed of progress.

---

## Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the resolution, you can escalate the complaint:

**Level 1:** Customer support (first contact).
**Level 2:** Support manager (after 10 days or if you are not satisfied).
**Level 3:** Management (serious or unresolved complaints).

To escalate, write to [hello@bisbi.io](mailto:hello@bisbi.io) with the subject "Escalation request" and include your case number.

---

## Regulatory authorities

If we cannot resolve your complaint, you may contact:

- **USA:** FTC ([ftc.gov](https://www.ftc.gov)), BBB ([bbb.org](https://www.bbb.org)).
- **EU:** your local consumer-protection authority or ECC-Net.
- **United Kingdom:** Citizens Advice.
- **Argentina and other LATAM countries:** local consumer-protection authorities of your jurisdiction.

---

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Receive fair and respectful treatment.
- Have your complaint investigated diligently.
- Receive clear communication about the status of the complaint.
- Escalate the case if you are not satisfied.
- Resort to external dispute-resolution channels.

**We will not:**

- Retaliate for filing a complaint.
- Suspend your account just for complaining.
- Share the details of your complaint without your consent (except where required by law).
